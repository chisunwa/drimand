Proprietary 5-Phase Vetting

Every DRIMAND chauffeur goes through an uncompromising multi-step security standard.

Criminal records, NIN verification, and local law enforcement checks.

Telematics Tracking
Continuous real-time GPS speed, route, and trip telematics monitoring.
